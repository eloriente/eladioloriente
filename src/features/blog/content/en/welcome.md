After several years working on projects for banking, automotive, aerospace and tax, I wanted a single place to gather my experience, my side projects and what I keep learning.

## What you'll find here

- **Experience**: the projects and clients I've worked with and the technologies I've used.
- **Projects**: side projects I'm working on or have worked on.
- **Blog and events**: articles, technical notes and events I take part in.

The site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build), TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, and it's available in Spanish and English.
